# Cyberpunk

See also: [Solarpunk](#solarpunk).

When I first heard of Cyberpunk, I thought it was simply an aesthetic and nothing more: neon lights in crowded technological cities. But then I watched [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=25Wzlg5See8) about how São Paulo is arguably the most Cyberpunk city in earth, and that sparked my curiosity. After watching that video, doing some research about the history of the genre and reading the excellent "[Neuromancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuromancer)", by William Gibson, I'd argue Cyberpunk is much more about politics than about aesthetics and neon lights:

- It's about how uncontrolled growth leads to the destruction of nature;
- It's about what happens when the world is ruled by very powerful corporations in the hands of few people, in contrast to the vast majority of the population being part of a poor underclass that lives off of small jobs here and there;
- It's about AI becoming smarter than humans, and what happens to those who control versus get controlled by the AI;
- It's about everything being an asset you assign a value to in order to sell, buy or trade. Your consciousness, your organs, your sexuality, all things have a price.
- It's about surveillance, cameras and robots that can track every individual, their preferences, beliefs and manners.

Cyberpunk is dystopian. And it makes you think about what world we're headed towards.

If you haven't read Neuromancer yet, please do yourself a favor and go read it.

# Solarpunk

Much like [Cyberpunk](#cyberpunk), Solarpunk is a science-fiction genre about the future of the world. But that is pretty much where their similarities end. Solarpunk has a totally different political goal: to optimistically dream of a world where humans have figured out how to use technology and science to live in harmony with nature, in a peaceful world where the collapse of nature and humanity has been revertedand is now a ghost of the past.

In my personal opinion, Solarpunk and Cyberpunk compliment each other. While Cyberpunk warns us of what could happen if humanity doesn't change course, Solarpunk optimistically reminds us that many of the world's issues are man-made and can be solved.

If you're curious about Solarpunk, I'd recommend the [Solarpunk Manifesto](https://re-des.org/a-solarpunk-manifesto/), the [Solarpunks](https://solarpunks.net/) website and [this blog post](https://www.scifiideas.com/posts/solarpunk-we-are-golden-and-our-future-is-bright/).
